# Surface: home page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a Zcash holder (first), a committee member weighing custody (second), usually on a phone.
Action: install from Google Play (QR on a computer, the listing on a phone; APK and iPhone waitlist on /download). Proof: the app's own components moving through one payment, the real testnet transaction, open source, the audit status in the FAQ.
Constraints: light paper ground; Verdigris + gold rules; app fonts; strict CSP, one same-origin script (loops.js, pause buttons); reduced motion shows stills; no 3D teaser (that lives on /showcase).

## Direction contract (2026-10-23)

THESIS: One room, real cards. Each heading says what its loop shows (no slogans). Each step of the story is a drawn scene of the same room that moves with one component the app renders itself, timed to it (the stamp seals the envelope as the card shows "Approved"; the tube takes the capsule at "Sent!"; three keys turn at "Vault keys ready"; at dusk the vault rests with a request waiting).

OWN-WORLD: Editorial illustration made in Blender (toon bands from a hand-picked colour script, tinted Line Art ink, object-space grain, window light), never generated imagery and never the app's own art. The app's cards sit on top, inked, with a flat offset shadow; a paper grain lies over the drawing only. Paper (#F1F5F5) around it, teal only on actions.

STORY: The first screen: a Google Play status badge, "The Zcash multisig nobody can see", one line, "Get Zafe"; the platform tab sits just above the fold. Then four steps (H2, one line, a loop): Your phone checks every payment; Two of three approve. It's sent. (with the real testnet payment); One key, never in one place; Quiet until it needs you (then the shipped features). Then Get Zafe and the questions.

FORM: Centre-focused single column; loops 16:9, a 9:16 cut on phones and portrait tablets; short single-moment loops (4-5 s), each with a pause button. Details and the pipeline: docs/site.md §6, DESIGN.md.

FINISH: checked at phone, tablet and desktop sizes on every page; the loops play at each size; DESIGN.md records what ships.
