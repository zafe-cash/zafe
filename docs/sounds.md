# Payment sounds

Zafe plays a sound and haptic taps at the moments a payment goes through. Decided
2026-10-06 after three rounds of listening (UI chimes, "tactile" bass, UI note runs); the
user picked the payment signature below.

## The set

One signature, three strikes on open intervals: E6, B6, E7 (a fifth, then a fourth),
struck metal with a coin's tick. Each moment plays part of it, so the sound follows the
multisig: your key turns, enough keys have turned, the money moves.

| Moment | Plays | Length | Haptic taps (ms) | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| approve | first strike | 0.6 s | 0 | proposal page, after approving |
| ready | two strikes (this approval completed the signatures) | 0.8 s | 0, 120 | proposal page |
| sent | all three, weight underneath, coins settling | 1.3 s | 0, 130, 260 | sending screen, success |
| received | top two strikes + coins | 1 s | 0, 110 | money arriving while the app is open |
| failed | the same metal muted, twice | 0.4 s | 0 (REJECT) | sending screen, failure |

- Generated by `scripts/sounds/sounds.py` (pure Python synthesis, deterministic; no
  samples, so no licences); `scripts/sounds/build.sh` writes
  `app/android/app/src/main/res/raw/pay_*.ogg` (~50 KB in all).
- Android: `xyz.zafe/payment_feedback` in `MainActivity` (SoundPool,
  `USAGE_ASSISTANCE_SONIFICATION`; no sound unless the ringer is in normal mode; taps
  via `performHapticFeedback`, which follows the system's touch-feedback setting).
  Dart: `core/feedback/payment_feedback.dart`; setting "Payment sounds" (Settings,
  `zafe_payment_sounds`, default on). Haptics play even with sounds off.
- iOS has no handler yet: Dart falls back to haptic taps only. It needs an AVAudioPlayer
  (or `AudioServicesPlaySystemSound`) handler and CAF/M4A copies (iOS can't play Ogg).
- Not played: by background checks, for other members' sends, for history found by a
  long sync (received counts only unmined or ≤ 2 confirmations, and never on the first
  load or a vault switch).

## Research (why it sounds like this)

UI sounds (Material, Facebook, sound-design guides):
- Phone speakers play almost nothing under ~300 Hz: keep the character in 500 Hz-4 kHz.
  Measured: Google's Material hero sounds have 0% energy under 300 Hz, centroid
  1-1.5 kHz.
- Confirmations 50-500 ms, celebrations 0.6-1 s; a tail that overlaps the next action
  makes the app feel slow.
- Material celebrations are fast rising runs (3 notes ~65 ms apart); errors fall.
- Layer each sound: transient (touch), body (pitch), short tail (space).
- One family (timbre, key), a hierarchy (bigger moment = more notes), harmonically
  simple so it survives repetition; test on the phone, pair with haptics.

Payments are different: a signature that says money moved, not UI feedback.
- Apple Pay: two clean strikes ~140 ms apart rising an octave (~1.16 → 2.35 kHz, no
  bass). Apple started from "what does money sound like?", rejected a candidate as "too
  happy and frivolous", and found two simple haptic taps in the sound's tempo beat
  mimicking its waveform (WWDC19 810).
- Mastercard: a 1.3 s, six-note cut of its sonic logo at checkout; 3.4x trust in their
  tests. Visa: under a second, "energetic, optimistic", chosen through focus groups;
  people were 12% more likely to say the merchant cares for their security.
- So: a short ownable motif, clean struck tones, open intervals (thirds read as playful),
  haptics in the same tempo.
